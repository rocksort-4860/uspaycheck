Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Short version: US Paycheck does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. We do not operate a server, we have no user accounts, and we cannot see your salary numbers, your location, or anything you type.

1. Data we do NOT collect

US Paycheck is designed to work without any network connection. The app:

  • Does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • Does not require you to create an account or log in.
  • Does not track your behavior, sessions, or in‑app events.
  • Does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash‑reporting SDKs.
  • Does not access your location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or health data.
  • Does not transmit the numbers you enter (gross pay, ZIP, tax year, filing status) off your device.

2. Where your data lives

Everything you enter into US Paycheck stays in the app's local sandbox on your iPhone. It is not sent to us. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

You may, through your own device settings, enable iCloud Backup. If you do, iOS will include this app's local data in your personal iCloud backup. That backup is encrypted end‑to‑end by Apple under your Apple ID. We have no access to it, no keys to it, and no ability to read it. Turning iCloud Backup on or off is entirely your choice and is managed in iOS Settings.

3. Tax‑rule update network access

US Paycheck works fully offline by default. The app will only access the internet if you manually tap the “Update Tax Rules” button inside the application. When triggered, the app downloads publicly‑available static tax‑rate files to refresh local calculation tables.

No personal data, payroll inputs, device identifiers or any user‑generated information is uploaded or sent outward during this network request. All your calculation data remains stored locally on your device only.

4. Purchases and restoring Pro

US Paycheck Pro is a one‑time, non‑consumable in‑app purchase sold through Apple's App Store.

  • The purchase itself, your Apple ID, and your payment information are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your credit card number, your Apple ID email, or your full billing history.
  • To "restore purchase" on a new device, the app asks Apple's StoreKit framework (not us) to check whether the signed‑in Apple ID already owns the pro_lifetime product. The only identifier involved is the Apple ID you are already signed into on your device, and the product ID pro_lifetime — which is a fixed, public product name, not personal to you.
  • Your Pro unlock state is stored as a simple boolean on your device (and, if iCloud Backup is on, in your own iCloud backup as described above). We do not maintain a server‑side record of who has bought Pro.

5. Children's privacy

US Paycheck is intended for adults calculating their own paychecks and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, COPPA‑style deletion requests have nothing to act on.

6. Your rights (CCPA / GDPR / equivalent)

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, export, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you would still like written confirmation of this, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

7. Third parties

We do not share data with any third parties, because there is no data to share. The only third‑party interaction in the app is the system‑level connection to Apple's App Store / StoreKit when you choose to buy or restore Pro, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change the app in a way that requires data collection (for example, adding a server‑backed feature), we will update this page, bump the "last updated" date, and — where required by law — show you an in‑app notice before the change takes effect.
