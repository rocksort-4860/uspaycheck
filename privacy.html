Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 13 日

In short: everything stays on your device

一句话总结：一切数据都留在你的设备上

US Paycheck has no account system, no server and no cloud sync. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting. Every calculation runs locally, and the numbers you enter never leave your phone. The only network access in the entire app is the optional "Update Tax Rules" button, which downloads public tax-rate files and sends nothing about you. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

US Paycheck 没有账号体系、没有服务器、也没有云同步。应用里没有统计 SDK、没有广告标识符，也没有崩溃上报。所有计算都在本地完成，你输入的数字绝不会离开你的手机。整个应用唯一的联网入口是可选的「更新税则」按钮——它只下载公开的税率文件，不发送任何关于你的信息。购买完全由 Apple 处理。

1. What we collect

1. 我们收集什么

The complete list of personal data we collect: none. US Paycheck is designed to work without any network connection and without knowing anything about you.

我们收集的个人数据完整清单：没有。US Paycheck 的设计目标就是在完全离线、对你一无所知的情况下工作。

  • No account, no sign-up. The App has no login. There is no name, email address, phone number, or billing address required from you.
  • 没有账号，无需注册。本应用没有登录流程。不需要你提供姓名、邮箱地址、电话号码或账单地址。
  • No analytics, no tracking. The App contains no third-party analytics, attribution, usage-reporting or crash-reporting SDK. We do not track your behavior, sessions or in-app events.
  • 没有统计，没有追踪。本应用不含任何第三方统计分析、归因、使用上报或崩溃上报 SDK。我们不追踪你的行为、会话或应用内事件。
  • No advertising identifiers. We do not request, read or transmit an IDFA or any advertising identifier, and we do not show ads.
  • 没有广告标识符。我们不读取、不请求、不传输 IDFA 或任何广告标识符，也不投放任何广告。
  • Your inputs never leave the device. The numbers you enter — gross pay, state, ZIP code, tax year, filing status, 401(k) and HSA contributions — are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • 你输入的内容绝不离开设备。你输入的所有数字——毛薪、所在州、ZIP 邮编、税年、申报状态、401(k) 与 HSA 缴存额——完全在你的设备上处理，绝不会传输给我们或任何其他人。
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's purposes — there is no data to sell or share.
  • 不出售、不共享。我们不出售你的数据，也不会把它共享给他人使用——本来就没有可出售或共享的数据。

2. Where your data is stored

2. 你的数据存在哪里

  • Local sandbox only. Everything you enter is processed and kept in the App's private storage area on your iPhone. We operate no server for this content, and we cannot read it.
  • 只在本地沙盒。你输入的一切都在本应用于 iPhone 上的专属存储区域内处理与保存。我们没有为这些内容运营任何服务器，也无法读取它们。
  • No cloud sync. US Paycheck does not sync your data between devices, and we store nothing about you anywhere.
  • 没有云同步。US Paycheck 不会在多台设备之间同步你的数据，我们在任何地方都不保存关于你的任何信息。
  • Device backups are Apple's. If you have enabled iCloud Backup on your iPhone, that device backup — including this App's local data — is created and managed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, encrypted end-to-end under your Apple ID. We have no access to it and no keys to it. Turning iCloud Backup on or off is entirely your choice, managed in iOS Settings.
  • 设备备份由 Apple 管理。若你在 iPhone 上开启了 iCloud 备份，该设备备份（包括本应用的本地数据）由 Apple 依其自身隐私政策创建与管理，并在你的 Apple ID 下端到端加密。我们既无法访问它，也没有它的密钥。是否开启 iCloud 备份完全由你决定，可在 iOS 设置中管理。
  • You control deletion. Uninstalling the App removes its local data. There is no copy on our side that could be restored, and no way for us to recover it for you.
  • 删除权在你手上。卸载本应用会移除其本地数据。我们这一侧没有任何副本，因此无法为你恢复，也无法代为找回。

3. Network access (tax-rule updates)

3. 网络访问（税则更新）

US Paycheck works fully offline by default. If you never tap the "Update Tax Rules" button, the App makes zero network requests.

US Paycheck 默认完全离线工作。如果你从不点击「更新税则」按钮，本应用不会发起任何网络请求。

  • The only network access is manual. The App will access the internet only if you personally tap the "Update Tax Rules" button inside the App.
  • 唯一的联网方式是手动触发。只有你亲自点击应用内的「更新税则」按钮时，本应用才会访问互联网。
  • What it downloads. When triggered, the App downloads publicly available static tax-rate files to refresh its local calculation tables (for example, new-year federal brackets or state rates).
  • 它下载什么。触发后，应用会下载公开可获取的静态税率文件，用于刷新本地计算表（例如新税年的联邦税率级距或州税率）。
  • What it sends: nothing. No personal data, payroll inputs, device identifiers, or any user-generated information is uploaded or sent outward during this request. All your calculation data remains stored locally on your device only.
  • 它发送什么：什么也不发送。整个请求过程中，不会上传或外发任何个人数据、薪资输入、设备标识符或任何用户生成的信息。你的全部计算数据只保存在你的设备本地。

4. Purchases and restoring Pro

4. 购买与恢复专业版

  • Apple handles all payment. US Paycheck Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ($3.99 USD, local pricing may vary) sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed by Apple; we never see your credit card number, your Apple ID email, or your billing history.
  • 支付完全由 Apple 处理。US Paycheck 专业版是一次性、非消耗型的应用内购买（3.99 美元，各地区定价可能不同），通过 Apple App Store 销售。支付由 Apple 处理；我们永远看不到你的银行卡号、Apple ID 邮箱或账单历史。
  • Restoring is a conversation with Apple, not with us. To restore Pro on a new device, the App asks Apple's StoreKit framework — not us — to check whether the Apple ID you are signed in with already owns the pro_lifetime product. The only identifiers involved are the Apple ID you are already signed into on your device and that fixed, public product name — nothing personal to you is sent to or stored by us.
  • 恢复购买是与 Apple、而不是与我们打交道。在新设备上恢复专业版时，应用请求的是 Apple 的 StoreKit 框架（而非我们）检查你当前登录的 Apple ID 是否已拥有 pro_lifetime 商品。其中涉及的唯一标识符是你设备上已登录的 Apple ID 和这个固定、公开的商品名——没有任何与你个人相关的信息被发送或存储在我们这里。
  • No server-side purchase records. Your Pro unlock state is stored as a simple flag on your device (and, if iCloud Backup is on, in your own encrypted iCloud backup as described above). We do not maintain any server-side record of who has bought Pro.
  • 没有服务端购买记录。你的专业版解锁状态只是保存在设备上的一个简单标记（若开启了 iCloud 备份，则如上文所述保存在你自己加密的 iCloud 备份中）。我们不保存任何关于谁购买了专业版的服务端记录。
  • No tracking, no advertising. Purchase information handled through Apple is used only to unlock and restore Pro. It is never used for tracking, profiling or advertising.
  • 不追踪、不用于广告。经由 Apple 处理的购买信息仅用于解锁与恢复专业版权益，绝不用于追踪、画像或广告。

5. Permissions

5. 权限

US Paycheck requests no permissions at launch — and in fact requests none at all. It never asks for camera, microphone, location, photo library, contacts, Bluetooth, calendar or health access.

US Paycheck 在启动时不申请任何权限——实际上它从头到尾不申请任何权限。相机、麦克风、位置、相册、通讯录、蓝牙、日历或健康数据的访问权限，它一概不会索要。

ZIP-based state detection is not location

基于 ZIP 的州识别不是定位

The App can confirm your state from a ZIP code you type in yourself. This is plain text input — the App never uses device location services and never learns where you actually are.

应用可以根据你亲手输入的 ZIP 邮编确认你所在的州。这只是普通的文字输入——应用绝不使用设备定位服务，也永远不知道你真实的位置。

6. Your rights and choices

6. 你的权利与选择

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, export, correct, or delete on your behalf under CCPA, GDPR or equivalent laws. Uninstalling the App deletes everything the App has stored. If you would still like written confirmation of this policy, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

由于我们不收集任何个人信息，依据 CCPA、GDPR 或同等法律，我们没有任何可以代你披露、导出、更正或删除的数据。卸载本应用即会删除它存储的全部内容。如果你仍希望获得本政策的书面确认，请给我们发邮件，我们会在 30 天内回复。

7. Children's privacy

7. 儿童隐私

US Paycheck is intended for adults calculating their own paychecks. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing to delete on a child's behalf.

US Paycheck 面向计算自己工资的成年人。本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在知情的情况下收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。由于本应用根本不收集数据，也就不存在需要代儿童删除的内容。

8. Third parties

8. 第三方

We do not share data with any third parties, because there is no data to share. The only third-party interaction in the App is the system-level connection to Apple's App Store / StoreKit when you choose to buy or restore Pro, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

我们不与任何第三方共享数据，因为根本没有可共享的数据。本应用唯一的第三方交互，是你选择购买或恢复专业版时，系统级别连接 Apple 的 App Store / StoreKit——这受 Apple 自身隐私政策管辖。
