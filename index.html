What it calculates

All math runs locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Federal Income Tax

2026 IRS withholding brackets from Rev. Proc. 2025-32. Single, married filing jointly, head of household.

State Income Tax

State-specific withholding for all 50 states, updated for January 1, 2026 state tax changes.

FICA & Medicare

2026 Social Security wage base ($184,500), Medicare rate, and the 0.9% additional Medicare surtax above the threshold.

Pre-tax Deductions

Model 401(k) contributions ($24,500 limit), HSA (individual $4,400 / family $8,750), and the SALT cap ($40,400) impact.

Pay Frequency

Hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly, or annual — pick what matches your paycheck.

ZIP-Based Locator

Type your ZIP to confirm your state before calculating. No location permission requested.

PDF Export (Pro)

Save or share a clean PDF of your estimate with one tap, via iOS share sheet.

Built on official sources

Not a random blog calculator. Every number traces to a primary source.

2026 data is sourced from:
  • IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 (issued Oct 9, 2025) — federal brackets, standard deduction
  • SSA announcement (Oct 24, 2025) — 2026 Social Security wage base
  • IRS 2026 retirement plan limits — 401(k) & HSA inflation adjustments
  • Tax Foundation, State Tax Changes Taking Effect January 1, 2026 (Dec 18, 2025)
  • Individual state revenue department announcements
